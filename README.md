# Ledger Starter for Claude (vn-ledger-starter)

Tired of telling every new chat the same rules? A ledger is a short Google Doc of rules that every Claude chat reads first. State a rule once, and every chat follows it.

## What it does
- Creates a Google Drive folder with a clean starter set: an index, general rules and a usage note.
- Adds a rule-writing method that replaces old lines instead of piling up duplicates.
- Teaches the cheap way to read ledgers (Drive text reader instead of the Docs reader), which saves usage.
- Includes a changed-ledger check and a model-and-effort guide.

## What it needs
The Google Drive connector in Claude. Nothing else to install.

## What it sends
Nothing except to your own Google Drive through the connector you already approved. No keys or passwords belong in ledgers.

## Part of a set
Works with claude-setup-wizard (switch on or off) and the other companion plugins.

## Data handling

It reads and writes your own rule documents in your own Google Drive. Whatever you put in them is yours and stays in your Drive. Nothing is sent to the authors of this plugin. We run no server and receive no data, so nothing is retained by us. The plugin sends nothing to any outside service. Everything it makes stays on your own PC or in your own Google Drive and Claude account, and you can delete it any time.
